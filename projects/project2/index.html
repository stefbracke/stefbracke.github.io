Canon AE-1

Documentation

An in-progress game-ready reconstruction of the Canon AE-1, focused on preserving the camera's mechanical character and recognizable silhouette.

Photogrammetry reference informs the proportions and surface detail. The production workflow covers hard-surface cleanup, efficient topology, UVs, baking, and a physically based material pass for real-time presentation.
